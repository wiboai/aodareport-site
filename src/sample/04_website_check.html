Website accessibility check

Northfield Components Inc. (sample) · www.example.com · Checked November 10, 2026 against WCAG 2.0 Level AA (excluding 1.2.4 and 1.2.5, as Ontario allows). This is a review of up to 10 pages, not a full audit or a certification.

Summary

We checked 8 pages. The site is in reasonable shape, with 2 priority 1 issues (the quote form cannot be submitted by keyboard; the careers page has unlabeled fields) that block some users and should be fixed first.

Method

  • Automated scan of each page (axe-core rules mapped to WCAG 2.0 A/AA).
  • Manual checks: keyboard-only navigation and visible focus, page titles and headings, link text, image alternative text, colour contrast, form labels and error messages, zoom to 200%, PDFs and video captions.
  • Screen reader spot-check on the home page and one form.
