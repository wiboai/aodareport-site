Northfield Components Inc. (sample) Multi-Year Accessibility Plan, 2026–2031

Prepared under the Integrated Accessibility Standards (O. Reg. 191/11). Posted on our website and available in an accessible format on request from accessibility@example.com. Approved November 14, 2026. We will review and update this plan at least once every five years; next review by November 2031.

Statement of commitment

Northfield Components Inc. (sample) is committed to meeting the needs of people with disabilities in a timely manner, and will do so by preventing and removing barriers to accessibility and by meeting our requirements under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005.

What we have done

Accessible customer service training was delivered in 2017 and 2020. A 2021 policy existed but had not been reviewed. The website was redesigned in 2023.
