This is documentation support, not legal advice. Your organization decides how to answer each question, and your certifier confirms the answers are accurate. Providing false or misleading information in an accessibility report is an offence under the AODA.

How to file

  1. Go to the Accessibility Compliance Reporting Portal: accessibilityreporting.ontario.ca. Select Sign In/Create Account, then New Submission → Accessibility Compliance Report.
  2. Have ready: legal name, business number (BN9) [client BN9], Ontario employee count (84 (61 full-time, 15 part-time, 8 seasonal)), business and mailing address, NAICS industry (332710 Machine shops), and the certifier's name, title and contact details.
  3. Answer the six questions below, add short comments where useful, certify and submit. Save the confirmation.
